# TERMINUS: Phases 3–13 (built)

> **Restyled (November 7):** TERMINUS is now a Victorian terminus on the same layout — iron train sheds, a brick station with a clock tower, lattice footbridges, old trains (the bullet trains are gone). What changed and why: [HERITAGE-plan.md](HERITAGE-plan.md). The pipeline, layout, levels, routes and gameplay numbers below still hold; the materials, props and trains described here are the first (modern) build's.

The concept and blueprint are in [PHASE1-2-concept-blueprint.md](PHASE1-2-concept-blueprint.md). Everything is generated by scripts in `scripts/railway/`, so the map can be changed and rebuilt. Nothing is modelled by hand.

## One command

```bash
sh scripts/railway/build_all.sh          # 192-sample bake, about 10 min on an M4
```

| Step | Script | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| generated textures (ballast) | `textures.mjs` | 11 |
| geometry | `rws_build.py --phase 5` | 3–10 |
| light | `rws_bake.py` | 11 |
| glb | `../airport/pcx_export.py` | 13 |
| game-ready glb (unlit, WebP, quantised) | `scripts/assets/prep-airport.mjs` | 13 |
| collision boxes and AI nav | `scripts/assets/voxel-map.mjs` | 12 |

**Outputs:**
- `src/assets/maps/railway.glb`
- `src/assets/maps/railway-data.json`
- the Blender files in `scripts/railway/out/` (open `rws_baked.blend` to look around)

**Preview renders:**

```bash
Blender -b scripts/railway/out/rws_baked.blend -P scripts/airport/pcx_render.py -- --viewset scripts/railway/rws_views.py --out scripts/railway/out/shots --look game
```

The views are player-eye cameras at every zone (`rws_views.py`): plan, bird, forecourt, lobby, gallery, the hall, the track bed, the train interior, the subway, the metro, the control room, the goods yard, the depot, the workshop, the road bridge, the throat and the car park.

**Working in Blender yourself:**
1. Open `rws_build.py` in Blender's Scripting tab.
2. Set `PHASE`.
3. Press Run Script.

To change the layout, edit the constants at the top of `rws_build.py` (`HALL`, `PLATFORMS`, `TRACKS`, `BRIDGES`, `SUB`, `METRO`, `GATES` …) and re-run. Don't edit the .blend, because the next build replaces it.

## How it's built

- **Helpers** are shared with LAST CALL (`scripts/airport/pcx_lib.py`):
  - `box`, `slab` (a floor with holes), `wall` (with door openings)
  - `glass_wall` (one breakable quad per pane), `stairs`, `rail` (balustrades), `cyl`, `strut`, `text`
- **One mesh per material per collection**: 135 meshes in all, so about 135 draw calls.
- **Collections:**

  | Collection | Contents |
  |---|---|
  | `RWS_10_Ground` | the levels |
  | `RWS_20_Hall` | vaults and columns |
  | `RWS_21_Platforms` | the platforms |
  | `RWS_22_Tracks` | tracks, the overhead line, signals |
  | `RWS_25_RoofGlass` | the vault glazing (`roof_roof_glass`: not breakable, the sun bakes through it) |
  | `RWS_30_Station` | the station building |
  | `RWS_31_NorthBuilding` | the north operations building |
  | `RWS_32_Galleries_Bridges` | galleries and footbridges |
  | `RWS_40_Underground` | subway and metro |
  | `RWS_50_Yards_Depot` | goods yard, depot, workshops |
  | `RWS_60_Trains` | all trains |
  | `RWS_70_Props` | props |

## Phase 3: blockout and dimensions

Levels (z, metres):

| Level | z |
|---|---|
| Underground | −7 |
| Track bed / yards | −1.2 (rail head −0.9) |
| Platforms / station / forecourt | 0 |
| Galleries / footbridges | +7 |
| Vault springing / crown | +12 / +18 |

Everything else follows the zone table in the blueprint. All movement dimensions keep to the game's limits:
- stairs have 0.18 m risers
- doors are 2.4 m tall or more
- corridors are 3 m wide or more
- the train aisles are 1.0 m wide

## Phase 4: main station architecture

- **Station building** (x 40–186, y 100–126):
  - a double-height lobby atrium under a glass lantern, and a glazed south facade with three entrances under a canopy
  - "PORT CALDER CENTRAL" on the facade
  - ticket counters and ticket machines, an information desk under a hung departures board, linked waiting seats
  - three security gateways to platform 1 (scanners, walk-through arches, lane rails), shops and a four-counter food court
  - escalators and two stair cores up to the +7 gallery, which has shops, seating at the balustrade over the tracks, and the admin offices
- **North operations building** (y 18–32), on two floors:
  - ground floor: staff, lockers, security, plant, the **control room** (consoles in front of a signalling wall), stores, a mess
  - upper floor: offices and the north gallery

## Phase 5: platforms and tracks

- **Platforms:**
  - P1 and P4 are side platforms, 9 m wide; P2 and P3 are islands, 10 m wide. All of them are 0.9 m above the rail head.
  - They have white coping and yellow tactile strips.
  - A ramp at each end runs down to the track bed, so the tracks are a route.
  - Each platform has a stair down to the subway.
- **Tracks:**
  - six tracks plus two goods sidings and the metro track
  - concrete sleepers every 0.65 m, 1.435 m gauge rails, buffer stops where they end
- **Overhead line:** lattice masts in the gaps between track pairs every 30 m, with cantilevers, a contact wire at +4.6 and a catenary at +5.6.
- **Signals:** colour-light signals at both throats (red / green lamps, emissive).
- **Roof:** three glazed barrel vaults with white steel arches every 5 m, purlins, tie rods and navy springing beams. White tree columns on the island platforms branch into the vaults, and glazed gable screens close the ends.
- **Footbridges:** two glazed bridges at +7 (x 72–78 and 142–148) with a truss on each side and a lit ceiling. Each has stairs down to P2 and P3; escalators from balconies off the galleries go down to P1 and P4.
- **Platform props** are spaced so that no lane runs more than about 20 m without cover:
  - benches, vending machines, double-sided departure screens
  - platform number boards, bins and luggage trolleys, a coffee kiosk on each island
  - clocks on the bridges

## Phase 7: underground

- **Subway passage** (x 112–120, floor −7, ceiling −3): it runs under all four platforms, with a stair from each and a column line, and it is lit by cool panels.
- **Plant rooms off the passage:**
  - the electrical room (cabinets, a transformer)
  - the ventilation room (two fans, ducts)
- **Emergency stair** up into the north building.
- **Metro hall** under the lobby:
  - a 13 m platform with columns, benches and signs, and the track in its trench
  - the tunnels at both ends are boarded up ("TUNNEL CLOSED")
  - ducts and cable trays on the ceiling
  - stairs and an escalator up into the lobby

## Phase 8: goods yard and depot

- **Goods yard:**
  - a warehouse with dock doors and rack aisles inside
  - an open goods shed with a loading dock over siding S2
  - container stacks and yard light masts
- **Depot:**
  - an inspection shed over T3 and T4: corrugated walls, roof skylights, raised walkways, yellow portal columns and an overhead crane
  - the workshops: machine tools, benches, tool chests, a rack aisle
  - a service yard with containers and a staff-only sign

## Phase 9: city

- **Forecourt:** a plaza with two buses at the bus bays, a bus shelter and a taxi rank with three taxis (the shaped vehicles from `pcx_vehicles.py`).
- **Streets:** a road with lane paint, pavements and street lights.
- **Car park:** eight cars in painted bays.
- **Park:** low-poly trees, benches and lamps.
- **Road bridge** (CALDER WAY) at +7 over the west throat:
  - parapets, street lights, two cars on it
  - reached by stairs from the park and from the goods yard, so it's a long elevated lane
- **Tunnel portal:** two sealed arches.
- **Beyond the fences:** a backdrop of towers, warehouses and a skyline.

## Phase 10: details

- **Signage:** platform numbers, PLATFORMS 1 – 4, SUBWAY · METRO, METRO M1, TICKETS, DEPARTURES, INFORMATION, the shop names, CONTROL ROOM, OPERATIONS CENTRE, DEPOT 3, WORKSHOPS, GOODS SHED 2, TAXI, BUS, NO ENTRY.
- **Light fittings:** ceiling panels everywhere the bake puts a lamp.

## Phase 11: materials and light

- **Textures:** the shared Poly Haven set (`scripts/airport/textures/pcx`, CC0), plus a generated **ballast** texture (`textures.mjs`). Flat colours cover the steel, liveries, seats and signs.
- **The bake** (`rws_bake.py`):
  - a mid-afternoon sun from the south-south-west, 48° up, and a blue sky
  - the vault glass lets the sun in
  - about 400 lamps at the panels: warm in the station, neutral in offices, cool and dim underground, high-bay lamps in the depot
  - soft lamps along the train ceilings
- **Result:** diffuse direct and indirect light, baked per vertex and tone-mapped. The game draws it unlit (texture × light), with no real-time lights or shadow maps.

## Phase 12: collision and multiplayer optimisation

- **Voxel collision:** 12.5 cm voxels from the same triangles, cropped to PLAY.
- **Glass:** a `--wall`. Breakable, with a clip wall that stays, so a broken pane is never a way out.
- **Fence and seat blocks:** `--soft`. They block movement only.
- **No hollow walls,** and every wall stands within 1.5 m of walkable floor (the voxeliser's mask). That rule exists because of the Underground stage's see-through-wall bug.
- **AI nav:** `STITCH` in `src/level/Railway.ts` adds walks down both trains' aisles and out of every open door.
- **Spawns:** single player starts on the forecourt with 26 soldiers spread over the whole map. Online, TDM uses the forecourt (Alpha) and the depot (Bravo), and FFA uses spawns from the nav graph.

## Phase 13: export and integration

- **Export:** `pcx_export.py` writes a glb with the baked layer as `COLOR_0` and the empties (`mission_start`, `mission_target`, `spawn_alpha`, `spawn_bravo`, `spawn_ffa_01…20`). `prep-airport.mjs` makes the materials unlit, the textures WebP at 1024 px and the geometry quantised.
- **Game:**

  | File | What it holds |
  |---|---|
  | `src/level/Railway.ts` (`buildRailway`) | the loader |
  | `config/stages.ts` | "TERMINUS", after LAST CALL |
  | `main.ts` | the thumbnail `assets/thumbs/railway.webp` |
  | `Game.load()` | the builder; the mark/fsb/swat soldiers |
  | `Sky.ts` | `SKY_CITY` |
  | `server/server.mjs` | the online rooms |

- **Test it:**
  - Mission: `npm run dev`, then open `http://localhost:5173/?quick=railway`
  - Online: `npm run server`, then add `&mp=ROOM&name=A`
