CONTROLS

TOUCH CONTROLS
Move — put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side: a stick appears under it. Push it far past its ring, forwards, to sprint.
Look — drag anywhere on the right side. The big FIRE button looks too while you hold it, so you can follow a target as you shoot.
FIRE (also a small one on the left) · AIM toggles the sights · RELOAD · JUMP (also skips cut-scenes)
CROUCH — tap to crouch, hold to go prone · NADE — tap to throw, hold for the next grenade type
KNIFE quick stab · SWAP next weapon · USE doors, pickups, defusing (hold) · map and pause at the top right
Button size and look sensitivity are in SETTINGS. Playing on a phone? Turn it sideways, and add the page to your home screen for full screen.
Click a key to change it, then press the new key or mouse button. ESC cancels · right-click a key to clear it. Changes are saved automatically.
